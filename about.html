A café built around the roast, not the logo

Ember & Oak started as a Saturday market stall with a 1kg sample roaster and a folding table. Four years on, the roaster is bigger, the table is a counter, and the Saturday-only part is the only thing that changed.

"We're not trying to be the biggest café in town. We're trying to be the one where the person behind the counter remembers your order."

How we got here

Four years, one drum roaster, and a growing list of farms we visit in person.
